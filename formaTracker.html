Privacy Policy for Forma Tracker

Last updated: July 2026

Forma Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") provides an offline body progress tracking app.

1. Data Collection & Processing

Forma Tracker is a fully offline application. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, health statistics, body measurements, progress photos, or daily logs to any external servers. All data you input remains locally on your mobile device's internal secure storage (Room Database and Preferences DataStore).

2. Data Backup & Security

Since your data is stored locally, deleting the app will erase your progress data unless you manually use the "Export Backup" feature. Backup files are stored locally on your device or in folders you specify. We do not have access to these backups.

3. Advertising

This app displays Google AdMob advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers or cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can manage your ad personalization settings via your Google Account or device settings.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer.
